Date Night Murder Mystery for Two

A murder mystery date night for two: London, Valentine's night, 1932. Lionel Sterling, owner of the glamorous Starlight Hotel, is found dead in his office during the Sweetheart Ball. The police can't get through the storm until dawn. That leaves you two.

Work together as a detective team, open three envelopes of clues, examine the evidence, crack a coded message and name the killer before the Midnight Waltz.

What's inside (25 pages)

  • How to play, kit checklist and envelope labels
  • Envelope 1, The Crime Scene: case briefing, illustrated office plan, doctor's report, victim + 5 suspect files, two detective casebooks
  • Envelope 2, The Statements: suspect statements, staff witnesses, lift log, switchboard log, photographer's plates, timeline worksheet
  • Envelope 3, The Evidence: 6 exhibits including a gold cufflink, two letters, a notebook and a coded blotter with decoder strip
  • 6 fold-over hint cards
  • 2 accusation slips and the sealed solution
  • 'Case Closed' certificate
  • 1930s date night menu, two cocktail recipes (one alcohol-free) and dress code

Good to know

  • 2 players, 60 to 90 minutes. One envelope per course works perfectly over dinner.
  • A fair-play mystery: every clue counts and sharp detectives can solve it.
  • Nothing outside the sealed solution gives the answer away, so either of you can set it up.
  • A4 and US Letter, each in full colour and ink-saver.
