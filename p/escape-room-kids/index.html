A printable escape room for kids and families, ages 8+, 2 to 6 players, about 45 to 60 minutes. A grown-up hosts with the easy guide and answer key.

What's inside (16 pages)

  • Grown-up's guide, answer key and 8 envelope labels
  • 6 hint cards
  • Read-aloud story and a Lock Sheet for recording codes
  • 6 different puzzles: a hidden-word letter, a toy-counting shelf, a paint-pot colour code, mirror writing, a cut-out jigsaw and a logic puzzle
  • The Music Box finale and a 'Master Toymakers' certificate

Good to know

  • Every puzzle works in the ink-saver version too (colour codes have matching symbols)
  • You'll need pencils, scissors and a mirror or phone screen
  • Great for birthday parties, sleepovers, rainy days, classrooms and family game night
  • A4 and US Letter, each in full colour and ink-saver
