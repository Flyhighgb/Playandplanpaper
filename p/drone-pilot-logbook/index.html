  • Site survey & risk assessment with sketch map

  • Flight log with totals that carry forward page to page

  • Training & skills log

Fillable PDFs

Type straight into the A4 or Letter fillable PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) or most PDF apps on iPad, PC and Mac. It includes 10 flight log pages ready to fill.
