What's inside (18 tabs)

  • Start Here: quick, friendly instructions
  • Setup: type your income, monthly budgets and savings goals once
  • Bills & Direct Debits: rent or mortgage, Council Tax, energy, broadband, TV Licence, insurance and subscriptions, with a tick box for every month
  • 12 monthly tabs: money in, bills, a spending log with drop-down categories, savings, and five summary tiles (money in, bills, spending, saved, left over)
  • Year Overview: every month side by side, spending by category, plus 'money in vs money out' and 'where the money went' charts
  • Savings Goals: progress bars that fill as you save
  • Debt Payoff: track credit cards, car finance and overdrafts as the balance falls

Good to know

  • Over budget? That category turns red so you spot it straight away
  • Start in any month, and rename any category you like
  • Includes a filled-in example file so you can see how it all works
  • Google Sheets: upload to Drive, then File > Save as Google Sheets
  • Works on phone, tablet or computer
  • A budgeting tool, not financial advice
