10 games to play at the Christmas dinner table

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: The best Christmas dinner table games need no moving and no mess: Christmas dinner bingo, table talk question cards, a cracker joke-off, Would You Rather and a quick 20-question quiz. Each takes 5 to 15 minutes and works for any age.

Between the starter and the pudding there's a lot of sitting. These games keep the conversation going while everyone stays put, and none of them need more than a pen.

1. Christmas dinner bingo

Hand out bingo cards filled with things that always happen at Christmas dinner: someone pulls a cracker too early, Grandad falls asleep, a sprout rolls off a plate. Tick them off as they happen. The first full line shouts "Bingo!" and wins the last roast potato.

2. Table talk questions

Put a question card at each place setting and go round the table between courses. Some to start with:

  • What's the best present you've ever been given?
  • Which Christmas film could you watch every year?
  • What would your perfect Christmas dinner have on it?
  • What's the funniest thing that's happened on a Christmas Day?
  • If you could spend Christmas anywhere in the world, where would it be?
  • What's one thing you're proud of from this year?
  • Which Christmas song would you happily never hear again?
  • What tradition would you add to our Christmas?

3. Cracker joke-off

Everyone reads their cracker joke aloud, and the table votes for the worst groaner. The winner gets to wear the gold paper hat. Need more jokes? Try our 40 cracker jokes.

4. Christmas Would You Rather

"Would you rather only eat sprouts or only eat Christmas pudding until New Year?" Everyone answers and explains. It's perfect for getting quieter relatives talking.

5. Quick Christmas quiz

Twenty questions read out between courses, scored on the back of a napkin. Use our 40 Christmas quiz questions with answers.

6. Guess the turkey time

Before dinner, everyone writes down when they think the turkey will reach the table. The closest guess wins a chocolate from the tin.

7. Two truths and a lie

Each person says three things about their year, and one of them is made up. The table guesses the lie.

8. Who am I?

Write a famous Christmas character on a sticky note and stick it on your neighbour's forehead. They ask yes or no questions until they guess who they are.

9. Christmas categories

Pick a letter. Everyone writes a Christmas food, film, song and present that start with it in one minute. Score a point for each answer nobody else wrote.

10. Compliment crackers

Everyone writes something kind about the person on their left, folds it up and puts it in a bowl. Take turns pulling one out and guessing who it's about.

FAQ

What games can you play at the Christmas dinner table?

Games that need no moving work best: Christmas dinner bingo, table talk questions, a cracker joke-off, Would You Rather and a quick quiz.

How do you keep kids entertained at Christmas dinner?

Christmas dinner bingo and Who am I? keep children busy, and picture-based games work for those who can't read yet.

When should you play games at Christmas dinner?

Between courses is best, especially while the main course is being cleared and pudding is on its way.
