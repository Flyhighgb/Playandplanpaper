25 Secret Santa questions to ask your coworkers

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Good Secret Santa questions cover favourite snacks and drinks, hobbies, allergies and anything someone definitely doesn't want. Ask 5 to 10 questions on a sign-up sheet, set a budget, and share the answers only with each person's Secret Santa.

Most disappointing Secret Santa gifts happen because the giver knew nothing about the person. A short questionnaire fixes that. Here are 25 questions to choose from, and a simple way to run the whole thing.

25 Secret Santa questions

  1. What's your go-to hot drink, and how do you take it?
  2. Sweet or savoury snacks?
  3. What's your favourite chocolate bar?
  4. Do you have any allergies or foods you avoid?
  5. What do you do to relax at the weekend?
  6. Name a hobby you'd like to try.
  7. Favourite colour?
  8. Candles: love them or leave them?
  9. Are you a book, film or podcast person?
  10. What's your favourite film genre?
  11. Is there an author or series you love?
  12. Desk plant: yes, no, or one you can't kill?
  13. What's on your desk that you couldn't work without?
  14. Mugs: do you have enough already?
  15. Cosy socks or fun socks?
  16. Do you have a favourite sports team?
  17. Coffee shop order?
  18. Any pets? What are their names?
  19. Gin, wine, beer, or alcohol-free?
  20. What's a small thing that always makes your day better?
  21. Are you more of a gadget person or a stationery person?
  22. Would you rather get something useful or something funny?
  23. Is there anything you definitely don't want?
  24. If you won a free afternoon off, what would you do with it?
  25. What's your favourite season of the year?

How to run a work Secret Santa

  1. Set a budget everyone is comfortable with. £10 to £15 or $15 to $20 is typical.
  2. Hand out a sign-up sheet with your chosen questions and a deadline.
  3. Draw names in secret. A hat works, or one person can assign them.
  4. Give each Santa only their person's answers.
  5. Pick a swap day, ideally at the Christmas party, and add a guessing game for the reveal.

Gift ideas that rarely miss

  • Their favourite chocolate or snack, plus a nice mug only if they said they want one
  • A book by an author they named
  • A plant they can't kill, like a succulent
  • Good stationery for the gadget-shy
  • A coffee shop gift card for their usual order

FAQ

What should I ask for Secret Santa?

Ask about favourite snacks and drinks, hobbies, allergies, colours and anything they don't want. Five to ten questions is plenty.

How much should you spend on a work Secret Santa?

Most workplaces set £10 to £15 or $15 to $20. Agree the amount up front so nobody overspends.

Is Secret Santa anonymous?

Usually yes, until the swap. Some teams add a guessing game where everyone tries to work out who their Santa was.
