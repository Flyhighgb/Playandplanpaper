8 Boxing Day games for the whole family

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 21 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Good Boxing Day games are easy and low effort: a Boxing Day quiz, Christmas film charades, a leftovers sandwich contest, a family walk with a spotting sheet, and quick 60-second challenges. Most use things you already have in the house.

Boxing Day is for staying in pyjamas and eating leftovers. These games fit right in, because none of them need a trip to the shops and most need only a pen.

8 Boxing Day games

  1. Leftovers bake-off judging. Everyone makes a sandwich from the leftovers and a panel scores it. Points for taste, looks and the silliest name.
  2. Boxing Day quiz. Ten questions on the year, ten on Christmas films and ten on the family. Mark them over a cup of tea.
  3. Christmas film charades. Act out a Christmas film with no talking. Works brilliantly with a mix of ages.
  4. New toy tournament. Turn the new board games and gadgets into a family tournament with a score sheet on the fridge.
  5. Walk and spot. Head out for a Boxing Day walk with a spotting sheet: a robin, a dog in a Christmas jumper, a wreath on a door.
  6. Emoji puzzle race. Put emoji song and film puzzles on the TV and race in teams.
  7. Year in review. Everyone answers the same questions about the year: best day, funniest moment, a goal for next year. Seal them away to open next Christmas.
  8. Beat the minute. Quick 60-second challenges using things in the house: stack the chocolates, flick the bauble, wrap a present one-handed.

Start a year in review tradition

A year in review is a lovely Boxing Day habit. Everyone fills in the same short sheet, you read them out, then seal them in an envelope to open next Christmas. Children love seeing how much they've changed.

FAQ

What do families do on Boxing Day?

Many families eat leftovers, go for a walk, watch films and play games together. A quiz or charades is a Boxing Day tradition in lots of UK homes.

What games can you play on Boxing Day with no prep?

Charades, Would You Rather, a leftovers sandwich contest and 60-second challenges all need no preparation.

Is Boxing Day celebrated in the US?

Boxing Day on 26 December is a holiday in the UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. It isn't a public holiday in the US, but these games work for any lazy day after Christmas.
