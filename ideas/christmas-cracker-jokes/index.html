40 Christmas cracker jokes for kids and adults (clean and groan-worthy)

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: The best Christmas cracker jokes are short puns that everyone can get, like "What do you call Santa when he stops moving? Santa Pause." Below are 40 clean ones: 20 for kids and 20 groaners for adults, ready to read out or slip into homemade crackers.

A cracker joke has one job: making the whole table groan at once. These are all clean, so they're safe to read out in front of children and grandparents alike.

20 Christmas jokes for kids

  1. What's a snowman's favourite drink? Iced tea.
  2. Why did the gingerbread man go to the doctor? He was feeling crumby.
  3. What's a sheep's favourite Christmas song? Fleece Navidad.
  4. Why was the Christmas tree bad at knitting? It kept dropping its needles.
  5. What do reindeer hang on their trees? Horn-aments.
  6. How does a snowman pay for things? With cold, hard cash.
  7. Why don't sprouts ever win at hide and seek? You can always smell where they are.
  8. What do you call a cat on the beach at Christmas? Sandy Claws.
  9. What do snowmen do at the weekend? They just chill.
  10. Why was the advent calendar so nervous? Its days were numbered.
  11. How does a snowman get to school? By icicle.
  12. What did the stamp say to the Christmas card? Stick with me and we'll go places.
  13. Why did Rudolph get a bad school report? He went down in history.
  14. What do you get if you cross a bell with a skunk? Jingle smells.
  15. Why did the tinsel get told off? It kept hanging around.
  16. What's Santa's favourite kind of music? Wrap.
  17. What do elves learn at school? The elf-abet.
  18. Why did the snowman call his dog Frost? Because Frost bites.
  19. What do you call a penguin in the desert? Lost.
  20. Who hides in the bakery at Christmas? A mince spy.

20 Christmas cracker jokes for adults

  1. What do you call Santa when he stops moving? Santa Pause.
  2. Why does Santa go down the chimney? Because it soots him.
  3. What did the wrapping paper say to the present? Don't worry, I've got you covered.
  4. Why did the turkey say no to pudding? It was already stuffed.
  5. Why was Santa's little helper feeling down? Low elf-esteem.
  6. What's every parent's favourite carol? Silent Night.
  7. How much did Santa pay for his sleigh? Nothing. It was on the house.
  8. What do you call an old snowman? Water.
  9. Why don't crabs give presents? They're shellfish.
  10. What did Adam say on 24 December? It's Christmas, Eve.
  11. Who brings presents to baby sharks? Santa Jaws.
  12. Why did the Christmas jumper need a lie down? It was coming apart at the seams.
  13. What do you call a reindeer with no eyes? No idea.
  14. Why did the snowman go through the bag of carrots? He was picking his nose.
  15. What do you get if you eat the decorations? Tinsel-itis.
  16. Why is it so cold at Christmas? Because it's Decemb-brrr.
  17. What do you call a snowman with a six-pack? An abdominal snowman.
  18. What do you call a broke Santa? Saint Nickel-less.
  19. What do you call a reindeer wearing earmuffs? Anything you like. He can't hear you.
  20. What's the best thing to put into a Christmas dinner? Your teeth.

How to make your own crackers

  1. Print joke cards and cut them out along the lines.
  2. Roll each joke with a paper hat and a small gift inside an empty kitchen roll tube.
  3. Wrap the tube in Christmas paper, twist both ends and tie them with ribbon.
  4. Add a cracker snap (sold in craft shops) if you want the bang.

FAQ

What is the most famous Christmas cracker joke?

One of the best known is "What do you call a cat on the beach at Christmas? Sandy Claws." It turns up in crackers every year.

Are these cracker jokes suitable for children?

Yes. All 40 are clean and safe to read out at a family table.

How do I put jokes in homemade crackers?

Print the jokes, cut them out, and roll one into each cracker tube with a paper hat and a small gift before you wrap and tie the ends.
