What to put in a Christmas Eve box: ideas for kids, teens and adults

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: A classic Christmas Eve box holds new pyjamas, hot chocolate and a mug, a Christmas film or book, a snack for Santa, reindeer food and a letter from Santa. Add a small activity for kids, or swap in grown-up treats for adults.

A Christmas Eve box is a small box opened on the night before Christmas. It sets up a cosy evening and gets everyone in pyjamas and in bed at a sensible time. Here's what to put in one.

The classic Christmas Eve box

  • New Christmas pyjamas
  • Hot chocolate sachets and marshmallows
  • A Christmas film to watch together
  • A Christmas book for bedtime
  • A plate tag and treat for Santa, and a carrot for the reindeer
  • Reindeer food: oats mixed with a little birdseed. Leave out glitter, which harms wildlife
  • A letter or a "nice list" certificate from Santa
  • Cosy socks

For toddlers

  • A soft toy
  • A board book
  • A bath toy for the Christmas Eve bath
  • Bedtime story about Santa

For kids aged 4 to 10

  • Colouring pages or a puzzle
  • A torch for spotting Santa's sleigh
  • A countdown sleep sheet
  • A small game for the evening

For teens

  • Fancy hot chocolate or a face mask
  • A new book or magazine
  • Fun socks
  • A voucher for the film they choose

For grown-ups

  • A good candle
  • Posh chocolates
  • A mini bottle of something festive
  • A crossword or puzzle book for the quiet hour

Tips for putting one together

  • Any sturdy box works. Wrap the lid separately so it can be opened easily.
  • Print a label with each child's name on it.
  • Set a small budget. The magic is in the evening, not the spend.

FAQ

What is a Christmas Eve box?

A small box of treats opened on Christmas Eve, usually with new pyjamas, hot chocolate and a film or book, to start a cosy evening before the big day.

When do you open a Christmas Eve box?

In the early evening of 24 December, before bath and bedtime.

What do you put in an adult Christmas Eve box?

Pyjamas or socks, a candle, posh chocolates, a mini drink and a puzzle book all work well.
