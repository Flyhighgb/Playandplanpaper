40 Christmas quiz questions and answers for families

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 8 min read

Quick answer: Here are 40 family-friendly Christmas quiz questions with answers, in four rounds of ten: films and TV, music, food and traditions, and general knowledge. Play in teams, read one round between courses, and give a point per correct answer.

A Christmas quiz fills an hour, gets every generation involved and needs nothing but paper and pens. Split into teams, pick a quizmaster, and give a point for each correct answer. The answers sit under each question, so cover them up if you're reading from a screen.

Round 1: Christmas films and TV

  1. In Home Alone, which city do the McCallisters fly to?
    Answer: Paris
  2. What is the name of the Grinch's dog?
    Answer: Max
  3. In Elf, what does Buddy pour on his spaghetti?
    Answer: Maple syrup
  4. What is the name of the snowman in Frozen?
    Answer: Olaf
  5. In It's a Wonderful Life, what is the name of George Bailey's guardian angel?
    Answer: Clarence
  6. In The Muppet Christmas Carol, who plays Bob Cratchit?
    Answer: Kermit the Frog
  7. In The Polar Express, what is the first gift of Christmas?
    Answer: A sleigh bell
  8. What colour is the Grinch?
    Answer: Green
  9. In Love Actually, what is the Prime Minister's first name?
    Answer: David
  10. Which Christmas film features the line "Keep the change, ya filthy animal"?
    Answer: Home Alone

Round 2: Christmas music

  1. Who sang All I Want for Christmas Is You in 1994?
    Answer: Mariah Carey
  2. Which band sang Fairytale of New York with Kirsty MacColl?
    Answer: The Pogues
  3. What was Wham!'s Christmas hit?
    Answer: Last Christmas
  4. In The Twelve Days of Christmas, how many geese are a-laying?
    Answer: Six
  5. What did my true love give to me on the first day of Christmas?
    Answer: A partridge in a pear tree
  6. Which Slade singer shouts "It's Christmas!"?
    Answer: Noddy Holder
  7. Who sang Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree in 1958?
    Answer: Brenda Lee
  8. In Jingle Bells, what kind of sleigh is it?
    Answer: A one-horse open sleigh
  9. Which charity single from 1984 asked "Do They Know It's Christmas?"
    Answer: Band Aid
  10. Which reindeer has a very shiny nose?
    Answer: Rudolph

Round 3: Food and traditions

  1. What is traditionally poured over a Christmas pudding and set alight?
    Answer: Brandy
  2. What is the German name for mulled wine?
    Answer: Glühwein
  3. Brussels sprouts are named after a city in which country?
    Answer: Belgium
  4. What is the name of the Italian Christmas bread with dried fruit?
    Answer: Panettone
  5. What were the three gifts of the Wise Men?
    Answer: Gold, frankincense and myrrh
  6. What is the date of Boxing Day?
    Answer: 26 December
  7. Who sent the first commercial Christmas card in 1843?
    Answer: Sir Henry Cole
  8. Which country gave us the bûche de Noël, or yule log cake?
    Answer: France
  9. Which royal helped make Christmas trees popular in Britain?
    Answer: Prince Albert
  10. What do people traditionally kiss under?
    Answer: Mistletoe

Round 4: General Christmas knowledge

  1. Who wrote A Christmas Carol?
    Answer: Charles Dickens
  2. What is the name of Scrooge's late business partner?
    Answer: Jacob Marley
  3. How many reindeer pull Santa's sleigh, including Rudolph?
    Answer: Nine
  4. Which of Santa's reindeer is named after the German word for thunder?
    Answer: Donner
  5. In which country is Rovaniemi, the official hometown of Santa Claus?
    Answer: Finland
  6. How many points does a snowflake usually have?
    Answer: Six
  7. Which London square gets a Christmas tree from Norway every year?
    Answer: Trafalgar Square
  8. Noël is the French word for what?
    Answer: Christmas
  9. What colour are mistletoe berries?
    Answer: White
  10. In which year was the Queen's Christmas message first shown on television?
    Answer: 1957

FAQ

How many questions should a Christmas quiz have?

Around 40 questions in four rounds of ten fills about an hour, which suits most families and parties.

What are easy Christmas quiz questions for kids?

Questions like "What is the name of the snowman in Frozen?" or "Which reindeer has a shiny nose?" are easy wins for children.

How do you run a Christmas quiz at home?

Split into teams, give each an answer sheet, read one round at a time, then swap sheets to mark them.
