12 Christmas games for large groups (10 to 50 people)

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 21 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: The best Christmas games for large groups are ones where everyone plays at once: a team quiz, Christmas bingo, Who's Most Likely To, the Left Right gift game and quick 60-second challenges. They need little setup, work for 10 to 50 people and suit mixed ages.

With a big group, the games that work are the ones where nobody waits for a turn. Everything below either plays in teams or gets the whole room involved at once. Each one needs a printer and a pen at most.

12 games that work for a crowd

  1. Christmas quiz in teams. Split into tables of 4 to 6, read ten questions a round and swap answer sheets to mark. Works for 10 people or 100. Printable version →
  2. Who's Most Likely To. Read a card out loud. On the count of three everyone points at the person most likely to do it. No props, no teams, instant laughs. Printable version →
  3. Christmas bingo. Everyone gets a different card and one person calls. It needs no skill, so children and grandparents can win together. Printable version →
  4. The Left Right gift game. Everyone holds a wrapped gift while you read a story. Each time you say left or right, the gifts move that way. Whoever holds a gift at the end keeps it.
  5. Beat the minute challenges. Teams take turns at 60-second challenges like stacking cups or flicking a bauble into a bowl. Everyone else cheers, which is half the fun. Printable version →
  6. Christmas charades. Two teams, a pile of cards and a one-minute timer. Films, songs and Christmas jobs like untangling the lights. Printable version →
  7. Guess the price. Show a list of Christmas favourites and everyone guesses what they cost. Closest guess wins the round. Great for mixed ages. Printable version →
  8. Emoji song and film quiz. Put emoji puzzles on the TV or hand out sheets. Teams race to name the Christmas song or film. Printable version →
  9. Gift exchange dice game. Everyone brings one wrapped gift. Roll a die each turn: swap, steal, unwrap or pass. Gifts change hands until the timer runs out. Printable version →
  10. Christmas Would You Rather. Read a question and everyone moves to one side of the room. Quick, silly and good for breaking the ice. Printable version →
  11. Pass the parcel with forfeits. Add a forfeit card under each layer: sing a carol, do your best reindeer, tell a cracker joke.
  12. Christmas word search race. Pin a giant word search on the wall and let teams hunt in turns. It keeps a big room busy for an hour. Printable version →

How to run games for a big group

  1. Pick three or four games and plan about 20 minutes for each.
  2. Make teams before people arrive. Mix ages and mix people who don't know each other.
  3. Print everything the night before, and keep one spare copy of each sheet.
  4. Give one person a microphone or a loud voice. Running a game is easier than playing it.
  5. Have a small prize for each game. Chocolate coins work well.

FAQ

What is a good Christmas game for 20 people?

A team Christmas quiz is the easiest. Split into four tables of five and read ten questions per round. Christmas bingo and Who's Most Likely To also work well for 20.

What Christmas games need no equipment?

Who's Most Likely To, Would You Rather and charades need nothing but the cards or a list of prompts.

How long should Christmas party games last?

About 20 minutes per game, and three or four games in an evening, keeps the energy up without dragging.
