15 Christmas scavenger hunt clues for kids (rhyming, indoor)

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: A Christmas scavenger hunt for kids uses rhyming clues hidden around the house, each one leading to the next, with a treat at the end. Below are 15 indoor clues with their hiding places. Pick 8 to 12 for a hunt that takes about 20 minutes.

A scavenger hunt is the best way to burn off some Christmas energy indoors. Each clue tells children where to find the next one. Start them with the first clue, and hide each of the others in the place the clue before it describes.

15 rhyming Christmas clues (with answers)

  1. I'm cold inside but full of treats,
    with milk and cheese and things to eat.
    Hide it in or near: Fridge
  2. I'm covered in baubles from top to toe,
    look under my branches, what's there? Ho ho!
    Hide it in or near: Christmas tree
  3. You fill me with bubbles and climb right in,
    look inside me, then you'll win.
    Hide it in or near: Bath
  4. I spin and I swish and I wash all your socks,
    open my door and you'll find the next box.
    Hide it in or near: Washing machine
  5. At night you rest your sleepy head
    on me. I'm soft and on your bed.
    Hide it in or near: Pillow
  6. You put your feet in me each day,
    check inside me straight away.
    Hide it in or near: Shoe
  7. I'm full of stories, old and new,
    look between my books for your next clue.
    Hide it in or near: Bookshelf
  8. Sit on me to watch TV,
    check under my cushions, what do you see?
    Hide it in or near: Sofa
  9. Wipe your feet before you come in,
    lift me up and look within.
    Hide it in or near: Front door mat
  10. I hold your toys when you're done playing,
    look inside me, no delaying!
    Hide it in or near: Toy box
  11. I'm full of pairs, but some have gone,
    open me up and carry on.
    Hide it in or near: Sock drawer
  12. I'm round and full of crunchy treats,
    the next clue's hiding with the sweets.
    Hide it in or near: Biscuit tin
  13. Look at me and who do you see?
    Someone clever, as clever as can be!
    Hide it in or near: Mirror
  14. Up and down you climb each day,
    check my bottom step, then on your way.
    Hide it in or near: Stairs
  15. I hold a plant that's green and tall,
    look behind me, by the wall.
    Hide it in or near: Plant pot

How to set up the hunt

  1. Choose 8 to 12 clues and decide the order.
  2. Hide each clue at the place the clue before it describes. The first clue goes straight into the children's hands.
  3. Put a small treat or present at the final hiding place.
  4. For mixed ages, pair older children with younger ones so everyone can read the clues.

Tips

  • Avoid hiding clues near the oven, fire or anything hot.
  • Take a photo of each hiding place so you remember where they are.

FAQ

What age is a Christmas scavenger hunt for?

Rhyming clue hunts suit children from about 5 to 11. Younger children can play with a grown-up reading the clues.

How many clues should a scavenger hunt have?

Eight to twelve clues takes about 20 minutes, which is perfect for most children.

What do you put at the end of a Christmas scavenger hunt?

A small treat, a chocolate coin, a Christmas Eve box or a present to open early all make a great finish.
