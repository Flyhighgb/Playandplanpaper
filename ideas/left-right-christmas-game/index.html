How to play the Left Right Christmas game (with a free short story)

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 15 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: To play the Left Right Christmas game, everyone sits in a circle holding a wrapped gift. One person reads a story aloud, and every time they say "left" or "right", everyone passes their gift that way. When the story ends, you keep the gift you're holding.

The Left Right game is the easiest gift swap there is. There's no stealing and no arguing, and it takes about five minutes. It works for a family Christmas, a classroom or the office party.

How to play

  1. Everyone brings one small wrapped gift. Agree a budget first, such as £5 or $10.
  2. Sit in a circle, each holding a gift.
  3. One person reads the story aloud, slowly.
  4. Every time the reader says LEFT, pass your gift to the left. Every time they say RIGHT, pass it to the right.
  5. When the story ends, open the gift you're holding.

Free short Left Right Christmas story

Read this aloud and pause a moment on each LEFT and RIGHT so people can pass.

It was Christmas Eve, and Mrs RIGHT was running late. She grabbed her coat, LEFT the house and then remembered she had LEFT the turkey in the oven. She ran RIGHT back inside.

"Is everything all RIGHT?" called Mr RIGHT from upstairs. "Nearly," she said, turning LEFT into the kitchen. The turkey was golden, the sprouts were RIGHT on time, and only one roast potato was LEFT on the tray because the dog had helped himself.

They loaded the car and set off. At the end of the road they turned LEFT, then RIGHT, then LEFT again, until Mr RIGHT admitted he wasn't sure which way was RIGHT. "Turn LEFT at the big tree," said Mrs RIGHT. "No, the other LEFT!"

At last they arrived. Grandma was waiting RIGHT by the door with mince pies. "You're RIGHT on time," she smiled. "There's nobody LEFT to come but you."

After dinner everyone sat down to open presents. Grandma looked to her LEFT, then to her RIGHT, and said, "Well, I hope you've all got the RIGHT present. Because the game is over, and whatever you're holding now is yours to keep!"

Tips for a smooth game

  • Read slowly. The faster you read, the more gifts end up on the floor.
  • For young children, use a soft toy or a bag of sweets in place of breakable gifts.
  • For big groups, split into two circles with one reader each.

FAQ

How many people do you need for the Left Right game?

It works with as few as 4 people and as many as 30. Above that, split into two circles.

How long does the Left Right game take?

About 5 minutes per story, depending on how slowly it's read.

What gifts work for the Left Right game?

Small, similar-value gifts work best: chocolates, socks, candles or a novelty mug. Set a budget so everyone brings something similar.
