8 printable Christmas games and activities for kids

By Play & Plan Paper · Updated 3 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer: Great printable Christmas games for kids are a rhyming scavenger hunt, picture bingo, charades and a letter to Santa. Picture-based games suit children who can't read yet, and clue hunts work best from about age 6.

The school holidays are long, and children get restless by about the 22nd. These printable games and activities keep them busy without a screen, and most need nothing more than paper and a pencil.

1. A Christmas scavenger hunt

Hide clue cards around the house. Each clue rhymes and leads to the next, with a treat at the end. Children can read the clues themselves from about age 6, and younger ones can hunt with a grown-up.

2. Picture bingo

Picture cards mean children who can't read yet can still play and win. Call out the pictures, cover them with sweets or buttons, and shout "Bingo!"

3. Christmas charades

Act out a snowman, a reindeer or wrapping a present. Little ones love being the actor, and there is no reading needed if you whisper the word.

4. Write a letter to Santa

A printed letter with prompts makes it easy for children to write their wish list. Add a reply from Santa on Christmas Eve for extra magic.

5. Advent activity cards

One small family activity for every day of December, like hot chocolate and a film, or making paper snowflakes. Pop a card in each advent door.

6. Mazes and spot the difference

Perfect for quiet time while dinner is cooking, or in a restaurant on the run-up to Christmas.

7. Christmas colouring pages

Colouring is calm, easy and works for every age. Print a stack and leave them on the table with crayons.

8. Draw It! with Christmas words

Kids draw a word while their team guesses. Easy words like "star" and "present" keep it fun for younger players.

FAQ

What age are printable Christmas games for?

Picture bingo and colouring suit ages 3 and up. Scavenger hunts and word games work best from about age 6.
