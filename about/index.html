About Play & Plan Paper

Play & Plan Paper is a small printables shop based in Manchester, England. We make party games and planners that you can buy, download and print in minutes, so the fun part of hosting starts sooner.

What makes our printables different

  • Every pack comes in A4 and US Letter, with UK spellings in the A4 edition and US spellings in the Letter edition.
  • There is an ink-saver version of every page, because colour ink is expensive.
  • Games are written to work for mixed ages, so nobody sits out.
  • Pages are checked by hand before they go in the shop.

How to buy

All our printables are sold through our Etsy shop, which handles payment and delivers your download straight away.

Get in touch

Questions, a problem with a file, or an idea for a game you'd like us to make? Message us on Etsy or email hello@playandplanpaper.com.
